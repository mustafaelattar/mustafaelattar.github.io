Companies I've built

Both companies start from the same observation: radiology AI usually fails at the point of integration, where the model meets the hospital's systems, the radiologist's worklist and the local patient population.

PACSVNAAI ORCHESTRATIONWORKLISTREPORTINGDICOM · HL7-FHIR

2024 – present

RAMYRO and ramOS

ramOS assembles the parts a radiology department already needs (image archive, vendor-neutral storage, worklists, reporting) and adds an orchestration layer that routes each study to the right AI model and returns results inside the reading workflow. Every AI output carries its provenance and confidence, and the radiologist makes the call.

Research

My group works where a method has to hold up outside its benchmark: on a different scanner, a different hospital, a population the training data never saw. More than 80 peer-reviewed papers so far.

Theme 1

Cardiac imaging and procedure planning

Automated segmentation, landmark detection and measurement from CT, CTA and MRI, from the TAVI planning tools of my PhD to cardiac MRI segmentation challenges.

Theme 2

Cancer imaging and diagnostic support

Lung nodules on chest X-ray, breast density and mass segmentation in mammography, skin lesions and histopathology.

Theme 3

Generalisable and trustworthy AI

Out-of-distribution robustness, zero-shot models for rare diseases, uncertainty and explainability. Contributor to the FUTURE-AI international consensus guideline.

Theme 4

AI for low-resource health systems

Testing whether models hold up in African clinical settings, and building the AFRICAI network so more of that work happens on the continent.
